# Uncle

`Uncle.png` and `Uncle.json` are the character of the Aseprite sample, made by the author with PixelLab and tagged in Aseprite.

They may be used and shared with Kwilos.js's samples and tests. They may not be used to train machine-learning models, and any use must respect the use restrictions in the [Open RAIL-M license](https://github.com/CompVis/stable-diffusion/blob/main/LICENSE) that PixelLab's terms refer to.
